Welcome to Auto Job Apply

Two quick things, then you're into Settings to fill in your profile and resumes. You can change any of this later.

1. Companion service

Paste the token from ~/.job-apply-project/secret.token (on Windows, that's usually C:\Users\<you>\.job-apply-project\secret.token) on the machine running the companion service.

2. Who is this browser for?

Applying on behalf of more than one person on this computer? Each named profile has its own contact info, work history, learned Q&A answers, and uploaded resumes. This choice is saved to THIS browser only - if you install the extension in another browser too, you'll pick a name there independently, and the two stay pinned to their own profiles even though both talk to the same companion service.
