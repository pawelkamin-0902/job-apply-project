Why this role exists:

The purpose of this role is to design and implement scalable software architectures while leading development projects to ensure timely, high-quality delivery. The role exists to drive continuous improvement in engineering practices, mentor team members, and collaborate with cross-functional teams to align developments with business goals. It also focuses on identifying and resolving complex technical challenges to ensure overall software reliability and performance.

What you'll drive:

Architecture and development

  • Designing and implementing scalable and efficient software architectures.

  • Writing, testing, and debugging complex code for new and existing software applications.

  • Delivering code to production in a timely manner according to business expectations.

Leadership and collaboration

  • Providing guidance and mentoring to junior engineers and team members.

  • Conducting thorough code reviews to ensure code quality, security, and best practices.

  • Leading and managing software development projects, ensuring timely delivery and quality.

  • Collaborating closely with cross-functional teams to align development efforts with business goals.

  • Communicating effectively with stakeholders, presenting technical concepts and project updates.

Engineering processes and quality assurance

  • Creating and maintaining comprehensive technical documentation for software development and system design.

  • Researching and implementing new technologies and methodologies to improve software performance and development processes.

  • Identifying and resolving complex software issues and technical challenges.

  • Developing and enforcing testing protocols to ensure software reliability and performance.

  • Driving continuous improvement in software development practices and processes.

What makes you a GR8 fit:

Must-have

  • Minimum of 5 years of experience in a similar senior engineering role;
  • Deep knowledge of JavaScript, TypeScript, React, HTML, CSS, state managers, build tools, package managers, and web testing frameworks;
  • Understanding of cloud computing, DevOps practices, CI/CD pipelines, containerization (Docker), and orchestration (Kubernetes);
  • Experience with relational and non-relational databases, SQL, and database optimization techniques;
  • Understanding of system analysis and design, microservice and monolith architectures, and resilient system patterns;
  • Knowledge of networking, security principles, authentication/authorization, and IAM concepts;
  • Experience with SDLC methodologies, version control (Git), testing frameworks, and code review processes;
  • Strong problem-solving, critical thinking, analytical skills, and decision-making;
  • Effective communication with both technical and non-technical audiences;
  • Understanding of browser internals, SPA/static web pages, client-server architecture, REST API, SSR, web security, and UI/UX patterns;
  • Ability to analyze code, debug complex issues, optimize performance, and develop proofs of concept (POC);
  • Upper-Intermediate English level.
